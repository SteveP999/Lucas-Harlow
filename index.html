Lucas Harlow

Lucas Harlow composes music that exists between silence and sound — where a single piano note can hold an entire afternoon.

His albums Daydreams and Yearning are invitations to slow down, breathe, and let the mind wander somewhere beautiful. New Age instrumental piano rooted in stillness, released through Hello Texas Records.
